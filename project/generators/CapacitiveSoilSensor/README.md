# CapacitiveSoilSensor

`capacitiveSoilSensor`: the black capacitive soil moisture sensor v1.2, 23 × 98 mm, ending in a point. The 555 timer, the 662K 3.3 V regulator and their passives sit at the top under the three pins (GND, VCC, AOUT) where its cable plugs in; the white line marks how deep it goes into the soil, and its name runs along the blade.

- `finish`: the board colour, black by default.

# Relay

The KS2E-class DPDT relay in its `plastic-amber` can, as photographed, 20.5 × 10 mm, with the part print and lot code on the lid and eight pins in two rows three pitches apart, so the can straddles the gutter of a breadboard: `NO2`, `NC2`, `P2`, `COIL2` along the top and `NO1`, `NC1`, `P1`, `COIL1` along the bottom. As on the data sheet's PCB layout, the contacts are two pitches apart and the coil three pitches past the common, 1.25 mm in from the can's end.

- The can is `data-hook="relay"` with `data-energised`; the editor sets it while the coil is driven, and a sound or motion cue can hang off it.
- Pin names come from the catalog's relay, so a wired project keeps its connections.

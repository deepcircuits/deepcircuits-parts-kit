# To92

A TO-92 part in elevation, flat face to the viewer: the black body with its rounded shoulders, the part print split before its digits, and three legs splayed from the real 1.27 mm to one pitch (DS18B20 by default: `GND`, `DQ`, `VCC`).

- `label` prints on the face; `pins` renames the legs left to right.
- `lm35Sensor` and `tmp36Sensor` are the LM35 and TMP36 analog temperature sensors in the catalog, legs `+VS`, `VOUT`, `GND` as their data sheets name them.

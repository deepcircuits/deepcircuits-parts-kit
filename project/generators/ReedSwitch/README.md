# ReedSwitch

`reedSwitch`: a glass reed switch lying flat, its leads eight pitches apart. The 14 mm envelope holds two flattened reeds, one from each end, their tips overlapping in the middle: apart while the switch is open, touching while it is closed.

- `closed` (state): the reeds touching, as a magnet near the envelope holds them.
- The pins are `1` and `2`, either way round.

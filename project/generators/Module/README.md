# Module

The breakout template, and the breakouts drawn from photographs. The generic `moduleTemplate` draws a Board in a finish, one Header of pads along the bottom edge with labels above, a silk frame and title, passives where there is room, and the part it carries (`chip`, `dual-can`, `can`, `sensor`, `dome`, `none`) at the real module's size; use it for a breakout the kit has no photograph of, such as the MPU6050 or a three-pin IR module. `width` and `height` in mm draw the board at its true size (the GY-521 MPU6050 is `width: 21, height: 16`); otherwise `widthPitches` and `heightPitches` set it, at least a pitch wider than the header.

Each board below was drawn from a photograph of the real part and has its own generator, with the catalog's pin names as its defaults:

- `dht22Sensor`: the bare AM2302, a 15.1 × 25.1 mm `plastic-white` housing: the tab at the top with the mounting hole and chamfered corners, the grille (three columns of square vents, and in the five rows under the first a slot each side, open through the side), the plain band with the print, and four legs (`VCC`, `SDA`, `NC`, `GND`). The catalog's DHT22 is the bare sensor, not a carrier board.
- `dht11Module`: the black 14 × 28 mm carrier with the `plastic-blue` housing at the far end (its grille drawn as the DHT22's), the plain hole under it, the power LED (`power-led`) and its resistor down the left, the pull-up on the right, and the three pins on the right-angle header standing past the near end, `GND`, `DATA`, `VCC` printed above the joints.
- `ultrasonicRanger`: the HC-SR04, blue 45 × 20 mm with a hole at each corner, the two 16 mm transducers (the aluminium can round its dark woven mesh), T and R by the bottom corners, the crystal at the top between the cans, the name under it, and the four pins (`VCC`, `TRIG`, `ECHO`, `GND`) on the right-angle header behind the board, standing 8.5 mm past its bottom edge with `Vcc`, `Trig`, `Echo`, `Gnd` printed upright above the joints. The drivers are on the back.
- `bmp180Board`: the blue five-pin module, 15 × 13 mm, its header down the left edge with the names printed beside it (`VCC`, `GND`, `SCL`, `SDA`, `3.3V` from the top), the 662K regulator and its passives down the middle, the hole top right and the metal-lid sensor bottom right. `variant: "gy68"` is the 10 × 12.5 mm GY-68 with four pins along the top edge (`SDA`, `SCL`, `GND`, `VCC` from the left as the front shows them; the names are printed on the back only), the passives and the 662K under them, the print, the sensor bottom left and the hole bottom right.
- `pirModule`: the HC-SR501 from the dome side, green 32 × 25 mm, the 21 mm Fresnel dome on its square base with its facet rings and cuts, a hole each side, and the three pins at the bottom edge: `GND`, `OUT`, `VCC` from the left, as the back prints them. The trimmers and the trigger jumper are on the back with the controller, so the dome side does not show them.
- `gasModuleTemplate`: blue 20 × 32 mm, the stainless mesh can on its black base ring filling the far end, two holes, `label` on the silk (`MQ-2` by default), four pins on the right-angle header standing 6 mm past the near end (`labels` for the print where it differs from `pins`). The comparator and trimmer sit on the back of the real board, so the top shows none. `indicators` (`{ power, out }`) draws the PWR and DO lamps on this side between the holes, `power-led` (`data-lit-state="power"`) and `out-led`, for an editor that lights them.
- `hx711Board`: the SparkFun red 30 × 24 mm amplifier, the load-cell pads (`RED`, `BLK`, `WHT`, `GRN`, `YLW`) down the left edge, `VDD` and the catalog's `VCC`, `DT`, `SCK`, `GND` down the right, the HX711 in SOIC-16 across the middle with two cans and the resistors round it.
- `microsdModule`: blue 42 × 24 mm, the card socket at the left, the 74LVC125 level shifter across the top and the AMS1117 regulator at the bottom among their passives, the six pins down the right edge in the module's order (`CS`, `SCK`, `DI` for MOSI, `DO` for MISO, `VCC`, `GND` from the top) and the catalog's card-detect pad `CD` below them.
- `rtcModule`: the Open-Smart blue 38 × 22 mm clock, the CR1220 cell in its gilded holder, the crystal can, the DS1307 in SOIC-8, the 472 array, the catalog's five pins down the right edge.
- Pin names are the data sheet's, in the order printed on the real board; labels of four characters or more stand on end. Every board carries the mark where it has room.
- The small boards sold with a right-angle header (the DHT11 module, the HC-SR04, the MQ module) are drawn with it: the black spacer at the board's edge and the pins standing past it, the pins at their tips.

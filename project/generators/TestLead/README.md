# TestLead

A multimeter's test lead from its plug to its probe tip, drawn from above at any two points: `testLead({ color, plug, tip, via, pose, leave })`. `color` is `black` (the lead in COM) or `red`; `plug` is the middle of the plug, which is the jack's pin; `tip` is where the probe's metal point is. The meter draws its own plugs (see the Multimeter card), so a lead starts exactly at a jack's pin.

- **The probe** (`probeTip({ color, x, y, angle, pose })`) is drawn in profile: the metal needle, the finger guard, the tapering handle with its grip rings, and the boot the cable leaves from. `angle` is in degrees clockwise from pointing right (90 points down, the default). `pose: 'touch'` (the default) rings the point where it touches; `'rest'` leaves the ring off for a probe lying on the bench. It returns `{ svg, point, tail, hit, box }`: `tail` is where the cable joins, 8 pitches behind the point; `hit` is the handle's outline as path data, from the guard to the tail, for a pointer target (the needle is left out so whatever it touches stays reachable); `box` is the area it covers.
- **The cable** (`leadCable({ color, from, to, via, leave, arrive })`) is a chain of curves through `from`, the `via` points and `to`. It leaves `from` along `leave` (90, down, by default) and enters `to` along `arrive` (its last stretch by default). A cable with no via points sags a little. It returns `{ svg, d, box }`, `d` being the middle of its stroke as path data, so a host can draw its own target along it. The cable is wider than a jumper wire, so a lead never reads as one, and its light line keeps the black cable visible on a dark page.
- **The whole lead** (`testLead`) points the probe along `tip.angle`, or else away from the cable's last point (the last via point, or the plug), so the handle always lies back toward its cable. It returns `{ svg, angle, cable, probe }`, the cable drawn under the probe. A host that draws two leads can draw both `cable.svg` first and then both `probe.svg`.
- **Rest places and routes.** `multimeterLeadRests()` gives `{ black, red }`, each a probe pose (`x`, `y`, `angle`) in the meter's pixels: below the meter, points outward, their tails meeting under COM. `multimeterLeadVia(jack, tip)` gives the points a cable passes on its way to a tip in the meter's pixels: none for a tip below the jacks, else past the meter's lower corner on the tip's side, and past its upper corner too for a tip above the meter, so the cable never lies across the display. Turn both into your own coordinates with the meter's placement.
- Hooks: the probe is `data-hook="probe-tip"` with `data-lead` and `data-pose`; the cable is `data-hook="lead-cable"` with `data-lead`. Neither carries `data-part`: a lead lies across other parts by design, so `check(artwork)` leaves it out.

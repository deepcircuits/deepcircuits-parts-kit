# WaterPump

The mini submersible pump lying flat: its 34 × 24 mm `plastic-black` body, the seam to the pump head at the right end with the head's inlet slots, and the 7.5 mm outlet pointing up. Two leads, red then black, leave the sealed left end for a Header of legs at the bottom edge (`M+`, `M-`).

- The body is `data-hook="pump"` with `data-running`; `running: true` also draws the water leaving the outlet in `lens-blue`, tagged `flow` (`data-hook="flow"`).
- `pins` renames the two leads.

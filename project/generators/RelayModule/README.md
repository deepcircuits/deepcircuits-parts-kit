# RelayModule

The one-channel relay module as photographed: a `pcb-red` 50 × 25 mm board with holes at the corners, the blue screw terminal (`NO`, `COM`, `NC`) at the left with its screws two pitches apart on the grid, the blue SRD-05VDC-SL-C relay with its print, the optocoupler, transistor and resistors at the right with the PWR and ON lamps, the high/low trigger jumper, and the catalog's control header (`VCC`, `GND`, `IN`) down the right edge.

- The lamps carry `data-lit-state` `power-led` and `status-led` with their `data-on-fill`; the editor lights them from the part's active states.
- `finish` recolours the board (the photographed one is red); `label: false` drops the silk line.

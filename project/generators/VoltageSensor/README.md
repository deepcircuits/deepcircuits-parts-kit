# VoltageSensor

`voltageSensorModule`: the red 0-25 V voltage sensor, 28 × 14 mm: the two-way screw terminal for the voltage to measure (VCC, GND) at the left, the 30 kΩ and 7.5 kΩ divider resistors and a hole in the middle, and the S, +, - header down the right edge.

- `finish`: the board colour, red by default.

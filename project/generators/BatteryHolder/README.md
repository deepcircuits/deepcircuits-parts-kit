# BatteryHolder

A battery holder seen from above at its true size, with a red and a black lead leaving its top edge. The leads end one pitch (+) and two pitches (-) from the left at the top edge whatever the holder, so a host can change the cells without moving a pin.

- `cells`: `2xAA`, `4xAA` (the default) or `6xAA` side by side in a black tray, `2x18650`, or `CR2032`, a coin cell in its round holder.

The cylindrical cells lie in series, each turned against its neighbour: a flat plate at the + end, a spring at the - end, and the tray's print along its bottom wall. No maker's name is printed on a cell.

# EspDevKit

The ESP32 DevKit V1 from the real board: 28.4 × 54.6 mm in `pcb-black` with the ESP-WROOM-32 module between the two 15-pin rows and its antenna at the top end, the USB bridge (QFN) and the regulator (SOT-223) below the module, the red power LED and the blue LED on D2, the EN and BOOT buttons either side of the micro USB at the bottom. `left` and `right` name the rows top to bottom, as the catalog reads them (`EN` and `D23` at the antenna end, `VIN` and `3V3` at the USB end).

- Hooks: `data-hook="power-led"` (`data-lit-state="power"`), `data-hook="user-led"` (the D2 LED), `data-hook="boot-button"`.
- The module's label is `chipLabel`; the board's name is `label`, printed between the LEDs and the buttons.
- `layout: 'devkitc'` draws the ESP32-DevKitC V4 instead, measured from Espressif's photograph of it: 27.9 × 54.6 mm, the 19-pin rows (headers J2 and J3) 1 inch apart running nearly end to end, the WROOM module over the first seven rows with its antenna 5 mm past the board's top edge, the regulator, the USB bridge and the red power LED below it, EN beside the left row and BOOT beside the right either side of the micro USB, and no user LED. Catalog names like `GND.1` print as `GND` and keep their full name on the pin.
- `layout: 'devkit-1'` draws Espressif's later DevKitM-1 and DevKitC-1 boards from their dimension drawings: 25.4 mm wide with the rows 0.9 inch apart, `length` (the board), `overhang` (the module's antenna past its top edge) and `firstPin` (the first row's distance from that edge) in millimetres, `module` (`mini`, `mini-s2` or `wroom`) over the first rows, the regulator, USB bridge (`bridge` prints its marking), RGB and power LEDs below it, and `usb` (`micro` between the buttons, `dual-micro` or `dual-c` under them) with `buttons` named left to right. A null in `left` or `right` is an unconnected place, drawn as a bare pad. Hooks: `data-hook="power-led"`, `data-hook="rgb-led"`, `data-hook="boot-button"`, `data-hook="reset-button"`.

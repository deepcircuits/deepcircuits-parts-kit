# Capacitor

A radial capacitor standing on the bench, seen from the front at the real size: an electrolytic can with its top lip, sleeve and negative stripe, or a ceramic disc with its code. Provide `kind` (`electrolytic` or `ceramic`) and, for a disc, `code` (the printed three digits).

- The 5 × 11 mm can is `cap-electrolytic` with a `cap-stripe` band carrying `−` marks on the negative side and a `+` on the other; a 30% shade makes the top lip and a 25% shade the sleeve's lower edge. Pins are `+` (left) and `-` (right, under the stripe).
- The disc is `cap-ceramic` with its code in `plastic-black-edge`; its legs bend in to the pitch. Pins are `1` and `2`.
- Legs are tinned wire one pitch apart. No value text: the host draws the `value-caption`.

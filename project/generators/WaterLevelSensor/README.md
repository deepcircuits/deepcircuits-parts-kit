# WaterLevelSensor

`waterLevelSensor`: the red water level sensor, 20 × 62 mm. Ten tracks run lengthwise over its far 40 mm, every other one joined along the top and the rest along the bottom; under them sit the transistor with its two resistors and the power LED with its resistor, and the three pins (S, +, -) stand on the right-angle header past the near end.

- `indicators.power` (state): the power LED, through the `power-led` hook.
- `finish`: the board colour, red by default.

# Max7219Matrix

The MAX7219 matrix module from its photographs. A single module (`chain: 1`) is the green board 33 × 51 mm with the MAX7219 in a DIP-24 above the 32 mm face, the five-pin input header standing out of the top edge (`CLK`, `CS`, `DIN`, `GND`, `V+`) and the output pins out of the bottom in the same order. A chain is the blue FC-16 board: `chain` (2 to 32) 32 mm faces side by side covering a board as long as they are (128 × 32 mm for four), with the right-angle headers soldered on its back at each end and their pins standing past the ends: the input at the right (`V+`, `GND`, `DIN`, `CS`, `CLK`), the output at the left (`V+.2`, `GND.2`, `DOUT`, `CS.2`, `CLK.2`). The board prints the names on its back, so a chain's front shows none; the pins carry them. Every face is an 8 × 8 grid of 3 mm LED wells at the real 3.8 mm pitch; face 0 is the one nearest `DIN`. `lit[face]` lists eight rows of `0` and `1`; `color` is the LED colour.

- Each face rect is `data-hook="face-N"` with `data-rows` and `data-cols`; each LED carries `data-led="face-row-col"` and `data-lit`. An unlit well is `indicator-off-light`, the pale grey of a resting LED, as the photographs show.
- The pin names and their order in `pins` are the same for both boards; only the positions move. A chain's two headers end at least 2 mm past the board and a whole number of pitches apart, so both stay on the grid.

# SensorModule

The small sensor boards sold by the hundred (KY and FC classes), drawn upright with their pins at the bottom and laid out as the real boards. The LM393 comparator boards have the element at the top and the header at the bottom, but their layouts differ and each follows its photograph. Flame and light share the FC board: 32 × 14 mm, blue, the element past the end on bent legs to the + and − pads, two brown `cap-mlcc` capacitors and two 103 resistors down the left, the trimmer right of the LM393, PWR under the trimmer, the hole beside the DO lamp, two 102 resistors down the right. The tilt switch is the 32 × 14 mm blue board: a row of four passives under the pads, the LM393 and trimmer side by side, both lamps by the header with their resistors above and the hole between. The sound sensors are the 34 × 15 mm KY-037 board, red: the capsule lying on its side past the end with its felt face outward, four resistors under it, the LM393 and the tall blue 3296 trimmer with its brass screw, the lamps flanking the hole by the header (`finish: 'blue'` for a blue one). The NTC and the IR receiver are the small KY boards: black, 15 × 18.5 mm, two holes at the far corners, one resistor, the title. Every board comes with a right-angle header: the black spacer on the board at its near end and the pins standing 6 mm past it, with the board's print above the pads (`labels` where it differs from `pins`: the flame board prints DO and AO, the KY-037 AO, G, +, DO). Each box is the board's width and runs from the element's tip to the pins' tips. Provide `pins` (the catalog's names, left to right), `element`, `comparator`, `indicators`, and `label` for the three-pin boards; each layout fixes its own size, so `widthPitches` and `heightPitches` are no longer read.

- Comparator-board elements: `flame` (the 5 mm black infrared LED lying along the board, 6 mm past its end, on bent legs to the + and − pads), `ldr` (the 5 mm photocell the same way, face up with its serpentine track), `mic` (the electret capsule lying on its side past the end, seen along its can with the felt face outward; `micMm` 9.7 by default, 6 for the small board). The comparator board also takes `cylinder` (the brass 4 × 12 mm SW-520D tilt switch standing out past the end on its two legs). KY-board elements: `bead` (the thermistor on its long legs from the pads in the middle, the bead past the far end), `ir` (the 38 kHz receiver standing at the far end, seen from above as the top of its body with the dome facing out, with the board's red indicator and its resistor).
- The comparator boards print no title, as the real ones do not; the pin names and the element say which board it is. The KY boards take `label`.
- Layouts were checked against product photographs: the flame board (user photo), KY-013 and KY-022 (ProtoSupplies), the SW-520D module (Components101), the four-pin LDR board (NotEnoughTech) and the KY-037 (45-in-1 kit docs).
- The KY boards print S and − on their headers (the middle pin is unmarked), and the order differs by board: the KY-013 (`bead`) reads S, +, − from the left (`OUT`, `VCC`, `GND` by default) and the KY-022 (`ir`) −, +, S (`GND`, `VCC`, `OUT`), as Joy-IT's photographs of both show.
- Indicators are the editor's hooks: `power-led` with `data-lit-state="power"` and `out-led` for the comparator's output.
- Every sensor in the catalog that is not a Module fits one of these: light (LDR), flame, big and small sound, NTC, tilt and the IR receiver. The gas sensor is the Module's `gasModuleTemplate`; the heartbeat sensor is PulseSensor.

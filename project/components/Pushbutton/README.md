# Pushbutton

A 12 mm tactile pushbutton at the real size: a 12 × 12 mm `plastic-black` body, a `metal` plate with its four tabs, legs out of two sides on the real 5 × 12.5 mm grid snapped to 2 × 5 pitches, and the 7 mm round cap with its shadow ring and highlight. Provide `pressed` and `cap` (`black`, a lens token name, or any CSS colour).

- Pressed, the cap shrinks to 90% and its shadow ring darkens; the cap carries `data-hook="cap"` and `data-pressed`.
- Pins are `1`, `1.2`, `2`, `2.2`: the switch's two contacts, each with its two legs joined inside (`1` and `1.2` across the top, `2` and `2.2` across the bottom).
- The whole cap is the pointer target in the editor; the artwork does not draw a hover state.
- The cover's clips sit on the two leg sides only, as on the 12 mm switch.
- `size: 6` is the 6 × 6 mm switch: the same drawing at half size, its legs three pitches by two so it sits across a breadboard's channel.

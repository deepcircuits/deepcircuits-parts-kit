# SlideSwitch

A slide switch: a `metal` frame, a dark slot and a `plastic-black` lever, three legs on the pitch. Provide `contact`: 1 or 3, the pin the middle pin joins.

- The lever is `data-hook="lever"` with `data-position`; the editor moves it by animating `x`.
- Pins are `1`, `2` (common) and `3`: contact 1 joins 1 and 2, contact 3 joins 2 and 3. `position` (1 or 2) is the option's old name.
